Direct Link
===========

Copy two things into the shared folder. Do not replace the store folder.
A computer that replaces store deletes the other apps already on the card.

Where it goes
-------------

The shared folder already has roms, banana and store. Leave those.
Add only the folder and the file from this zip:

    store/
      apps/
        direct-link/
          ui/
          wifi-link
          host.sh
          ...
      tiles/
        Direct Link.sh
      (other apps stay here)

Then reboot once and open Apps. The tile is Direct Link.

One handheld presses Host. The other presses Join.
In RetroArch or PPSSPP, type the host address shown on screen: 10.42.0.1.
There is no internet while the link is up. Disconnect brings the home Wi-Fi back.

One folder too deep
-------------------

The usual mistake is unzipping into the app's own folder, so the card has:

    store/apps/direct-link/direct-link/

Open the tile anyway. It says so, and A moves it up. Or move the inner
direct-link up yourself and put Direct Link.sh in store/tiles.

English
-------

1. On the console: Settings > Network > File sharing > On.
2. On the computer, open the share (the folder that already has roms, banana, store).
   Windows: \\<the console's IP> in Explorer.
   macOS: Cmd+K, smb://<the console's IP>. Copy into store. Do not replace store.
   Android (CX File Explorer): Network > New location > SMB.
   Or the microSD in a reader, on the BANANA partition.
3. Copy the direct-link folder to store/apps/ and Direct Link.sh to store/tiles/.
4. Reboot once. Open Apps > Direct Link.

Uninstall: in the app, Y, then A. That disconnects and deletes the app and the tile.

Español
-------

1. En la consola: Ajustes > Red > Compartir archivos > On.
2. En la computadora, abre la carpeta compartida (la que ya tiene roms, banana, store).
   Windows: \\<la IP de la consola> en el Explorador.
   macOS: Cmd+K, smb://<la IP>. Copia dentro de store. No reemplaces store.
   Android (CX File Explorer): Red > Nueva ubicación > SMB.
   O la microSD en un lector, en la partición BANANA.
3. Copia la carpeta direct-link a store/apps/ y Direct Link.sh a store/tiles/.
4. Reinicia una vez. Abre Apps > Direct Link.

Desinstalar: en la app, Y y luego A. Eso desconecta y borra la app y el tile.
